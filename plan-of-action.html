Strategy & Plan of Action · June 2026

Prove the demand first.
Then build the company.

Before we raise and scale, we run a lean, Meta-led test in Asia-Pacific — Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia — to learn which market, price and scope convert. Real leads, real money, real data, in 90 days.

01 — The approach

A Meta-led MVP, before a single euro of build spend.

We could spend a year building. Instead, we spend 90 days finding out if people will pay — using paid ads to put a real offer in front of real buyers and measuring exactly what happens.

Key point

We use the founders' existing assets to test demand cheaply in three markets at once, then put our money behind the one that proves itself.

WHY THIS WAY

Test before we build

No big upfront product. A simple offer, a landing page, and real ad spend tell us the truth faster than any plan.

WHY APAC FIRST

We already have reach there

Digital Movement has an Australian client base and agency capability. We start where we have warm relationships and credibility.

WHAT WE LEARN

Where the money is

Which market, which price, and which scope convert best — so we scale the winner instead of guessing.

02 — The three test markets

Hong Kong · Singapore · Australia.

Three English-speaking, finance-heavy, regulation-driven markets — so one set of creative works across all three, and the only real variable is demand.

🇭🇰

Hong Kong

A dense financial-services hub with high cyber anxiety and an active regulator (HKMA). A large, under-served mid-market that the big firms price out.

🇸🇬

Singapore

The fastest-growing cyber market of our long-term three, with strict rules (CSA, MAS TRM) and English throughout. The bridge between this test and our core thesis.

🇦🇺

Australia

Where Digital Movement already has a client base — warm leads and instant credibility. Strong privacy/breach-reporting pressure and healthy mid-market budgets.

Singapore also sits in our long-term core (Germany · UK · Singapore). The APAC test tells us whether to lead the scale-up from Asia or Europe.

03 — The sales funnel

From an ad to a paid audit — six clean steps.

Deliberately simple, fully tracked, and built so every step teaches us something about demand, price and scope.

Why no instant form

We send people from Meta to our own landing page — not a Meta instant form. Instant forms give cheap, low-intent leads. Our page filters for real intent, tells the full story, and tracks everything properly.

1

Meta Ads — one campaign per market

Separate, localised campaigns in Hong Kong, Singapore and Australia. We test creative, message and audience in each.

Owner: Raoul — creative + ad management
2

Dedicated .com landing page (one per market)

Each market gets its own landing page — local language nuance, local examples, the offer in full. No instant form; we redirect from Meta to here for control and clean tracking.

Owner: Raoul — web development
3

Google Calendar self-booking link

The page's one job: get the visitor to book a call. A self-service Google Calendar link lets them pick a time in seconds — no back-and-forth.

Owner: Raoul — funnel setup
4

Scheduled discovery & sales call

Martey runs the call: understand the prospect, qualify, present the audit, and agree price and scope. This is where we learn what buyers actually want and will pay.

Owner: Martey — sales
5

The audit

We deliver the AI-threat vulnerability audit — exposure score, findings, attack story, fix plan and compliance mapping (see the sample audit).

Owner: Abhi — technical delivery
6

Handoff & ongoing relationship

Abhi leads delivery operations; Raoul owns customer communication and the ongoing relationship — keeping the door open for the recurring service.

Owners: Abhi — ops · Raoul — comms

Three founders. No gaps in the funnel.

Each founder owns a stage of the funnel end-to-end, and each brings a real, existing asset to the table — which is why the test costs so little.

M

Growth & Sales

Runs Digital Movement's marketing agency and its Australian client base — a source of warm leads. Takes every booked sales call and owns the demand side.

R

Raoul

Brand, Funnel & Acquisition

Consultancy, full website & landing-page development, and Meta Ads creative + management. Builds the funnel and owns customer communication.

A

Senior technical lead and computer scientist who leads tech teams. Owns audit operations, delivery quality and the technical roadmap.

Why this team de-risks the test

The whole funnel is built and run in-house — so the only real cost is ad spend.

Most startups pay an agency to build the funnel and run the ads. We don't. Raoul builds the pages and runs Meta; Martey brings warm demand and closes; Abhi delivers. That keeps the MVP cheap and the learning fast.

06 — What we measure & decide

The data that picks the winning market.

Every market is judged on the same numbers. The one with the best, most affordable path from ad to paid audit wins our focus.

The whole point

By day 90 we know — with real numbers, not opinions — which market, which price and which scope to scale.

DECISION GATE · GREEN

Scale it

One market clears the targets — concentrate spend and build the repeatable funnel.

DECISION GATE · AMBER

Adjust

Promising but not there — change price, scope or creative and re-test for a defined period.

DECISION GATE · RED

Pivot

No market converts affordably — change the offer or the segment before spending more.

08 — From MVP to the full company

The test feeds straight into the bigger plan.

This isn't a side project — it's how we de-risk the company in the business plan and the financial model.

What the MVP proves

  • Real, paying demand — or an honest signal to change course.
  • The price the market will actually pay.
  • The audit scope buyers truly want.
  • Which market to lead the scale-up from.

What it unlocks next

  • A proven funnel to pour the seed raise into.
  • The move from one-off audits to the recurring service.
  • Expansion into the core regulated markets (Germany · UK · Singapore).
  • The first data points behind the financial model.
The next growth lever

Win the market once, then own its search results.

Once the MVP names the winning market, we layer on a full SEO strategy in that market, run by Digital Movement — capturing everyone searching for "NIS2 audit", "cyberattack vulnerability assessment" and the like. Paid Meta ads prove demand fast; SEO then compounds it cheaply, an owned asset that keeps producing leads long after the ad budget stops. It's a third acquisition channel, already in-house, switched on only once we know exactly where to point it.
