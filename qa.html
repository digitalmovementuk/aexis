Q1 · Martey12 Jun 2026
Services

What exact services will we offer — so a delivery team can be scoped?

"I was speaking with my Indian team and asked for a summary of what actual services we want to offer so they can see if they can build a team out for us. Do you guys have any thoughts around the specific services we'd be offering?"

Three services, in a deliberate order — the audit opens the account, the monthly service keeps it, the software scales it:

1 · SIGNAL — one-off audit

The AI-threat audit

4–6 weeks, fixed price. We mimic how an AI attacker breaks in, across: external attack surface, identity & access (incl. leaked passwords), email & people, cloud & infrastructure, suppliers, and the client's own AI use. Output: exposure score (0–100), a 90-day fix plan, a board report + technical pack, and findings mapped to NIS2 / DORA / CSA / MAS. Method aligned to OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK, PTES, NIST.

2 · SHIELD — monthly

Ongoing protection

We watch systems day and night, re-test as things change, keep compliance evidence current, send a short monthly report plus instant alerts, track every fix to done, and run a fresh check when a new threat appears.

3 · CONTINUUM — software

The platform (later)

A software service / API that does the repeated work automatically — attack simulation plus regulation tracking in one platform. Built as internal tooling first, opened to existing customers from Year 2.

What that means for a delivery bench — the skills to recruit for, in priority order:

  • Senior penetration testers (OSCP / CREST-grade) — run Signal end to end.
  • Cloud & identity security — Microsoft 365, AWS/Azure, access management; most mid-market findings live here.
  • External recon / OSINT — attack-surface mapping, leaked-credential hunting.
  • Monitoring / SOC capability — the day-and-night side of Shield.
  • AI engineers — automate the repetitive testing; later, build Continuum with Abhi.
Hiring rule

We hire against signed contracts only — the bench is scoped now, activated when revenue lands. No fixed payroll before the work exists.

Q2 · Martey12 Jun 2026
Pricing

What's the market price point — and which companies are worth checking?

"Keen to check out some existing company's websites to see what the average price point is."

We researched this for the pricing strategy. For a mid-market penetration test / security audit, published 2025 industry pricing guides (Bright Defense, DeepStrike) put the realistic range at $10,000–$50,000 per engagement, with firms of 150–500 staff typically budgeting $20k–$50k. That's the market we price against.

Worth a lookWhat they show us
Cobalt, Synack, Bishop Fox, NCC GroupClassic + platform-based pen testing — engagement scoping and how audits are packaged.
Pentera, Horizon3.aiAutomated attack simulation — the closest to our AI-led testing angle (and to Continuum).
Arctic Wolf, Huntress, Expel, Rapid7The monthly managed-protection side — how Shield-style subscriptions are presented and tiered.

Our price position (the strategic ramp, already in the Revenue tab): Year 1 entry at €7,500–9,500 per audit — deliberately below the typical procurement-approval threshold so a mid-market buyer can say yes fast — then ~€20,000 in Year 2 once referenceable, and €30,000–45,000 at market rate from Year 3.

Q3 · Martey12 Jun 2026
Business model

Is this a subscription business?

"I figure it would essentially be a subscription based service?"

Half of it — by design. The model is a one-off product that feeds a subscription:

  • Signal (one-off) is the way in — it creates trust, markets us, and pays for itself. Not a subscription, on purpose: a low-commitment first yes.
  • Shield (subscription) is the engine — the monthly retainer that compounds. The plan assumes roughly 45% of audit clients convert to it.
  • Continuum (subscription) adds software revenue on top from Year 2.
The arc

Year 1: audits lead. Year 2: Shield recurring becomes the engine. Year 3: recurring + software dominate, audits stay as the steady acquisition channel.

Q4 · Martey12 Jun 2026
Unit economics

If a subscription is under €1,000/month, doesn't that force huge client volume and a big team?

"Value wise if the subscriptions aren't worth on a monthly basis at least 1,000 in any currency… it'd need to be an extremely high client volume business which we'd most likely need to build a bigger team to service."

Your logic is right — which is exactly why we don't play there. Shield is not a sub-€1k tool subscription. The plan prices it as a serious managed-protection retainer for regulated mid-market firms (250–5,000 staff): the working assumption is ~€90,000 a year ≈ €7,500 a month, scaled to client size. That's 7–8× your €1k floor.

So the maths is low volume × high value, not high volume × low ticket. Plan-stage numbers (from the Revenue tab):

Year-1 targetVolume it impliesTeam it needs
~€450k from audits≈ 50–60 audits at €7.5–9.5k entry pricing~5 senior offshore experts at ~USD 2,500/month all-in — hired only against signed contracts
~€540k from Shield≈ 6 retainers at ~€90k each

One expert costs ~USD 30k a year and supports several hundred thousand euros of delivery — so the team grows with revenue, not ahead of it, and the plan stays profitable from Year 1 (~35% EBITDA). A bigger team only happens because signed contracts demand it — which is the good problem.

One caveat

These are plan-stage assumptions. The MVP's whole job is to test the price point and conversion in the real market — that's what the first audits in DE / UK / SG will tell us.
