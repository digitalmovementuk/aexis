Sales Funnel · AEXIS

The sales funnel — and whether it beats launching DM instead.

Every stage from ad to recurring revenue, the conversion numbers we'll measure, and the honest comparison Martey asked for: is an AEXIS client worth more than a digital-marketing client in the same market?

01 — The question this tab answers

Is AEXIS worth a new operation — or should DM just enter these markets?

"Any form of security would need to be ongoing, so a subscription based model could work. I'm intrigued as to what the value is for the services we'd be targeting — because if it's not equal to what we can offer digital marketing for, it would make more sense for me to launch into those markets with DM instead of undertaking a whole new operation." — Martey, 12 Jun 2026

Right instinct on both counts: security is ongoing (that's Shield), and the opportunity-cost question is the correct one to ask before committing. This tab lays out the funnel, the conversion paths, and the value comparison — so the decision is made on numbers, not enthusiasm.

WHY THE BUDGET IS FORCED

Security spend is mandatory; marketing is discretionary

NIS2, DORA, CSA and MAS make defence a legal requirement with board liability. A cyber audit is bought from the compliance budget — it survives downturns that cut marketing first.

PRICING POWER

A €30bn market the mid-tier can't buy from

The Big-4 price out the mid-market; local firms sell yesterday's manual testing. The buyer has budget and few good options — the opposite of the crowded agency market.

Honest caveat

These are plan assumptions, not results. The MVP exists to produce the real numbers in 90 days for ~€9k of ad spend — the cheapest possible way to answer this question with data instead of opinion.

06 — Competition · market · sales · business development

The four commercial lenses, one screen each.

COMPETITION

A thin middle, and we sit in it

Big-4 too expensive and slow for the mid-market; local pure-plays single-country and manual; AI-tool startups secure only the AI stack. Nobody combines AI-native testing + mid-market price + compliance evidence — the USP tab has the head-to-head.

MARKET

~€30bn, growing, regulation-forced

Germany: the biggest economy, ~$150bn/yr cyber damage to mid-sized firms. UK: 2,600+ relevant firms, new cyber law incoming. Singapore: fastest-growing, strictly regulated. Demand is created by law, not by persuasion.

SALES

Short cycle by design

Fixed entry price under the procurement-approval threshold, self-booked calls, fixed 4–6-week delivery. The audit is a low-commitment first yes; the regulation deadline does the urgency for us.

BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

Every audit opens three doors

The fix plan sells Shield; the report creates referrals (insurers, auditors, accountants who demand proof); the delivery data trains Continuum. Plus DM AU's warm client base as the first referral channel.

The bottom line for Martey

Same funnel skills you already have, a second product with a mandatory budget behind it, a client value of ~€48k expected in year one (plan), recurring after that — and a 90-day, €9k test that produces the real numbers before anyone commits to a "whole new operation."

07 — The question behind the question

What it takes to be worth €10 million.

"What do we need to become a €10M-valued company, realistically?" — the question behind the DM-vs-AEXIS comparison.

Service and security businesses are valued two main ways — and the plan already maps onto both. Rule-of-thumb multiples (validate with an advisor at the time): security/managed services trade at roughly 6–10× EBITDA, recurring security revenue at roughly 2–4× ARR, software meaningfully higher.

Route to €10MWhat it requiresWhere the plan stands
Profit route — ~7× EBITDA~€1.4M EBITDAThe plan's Year 2: €3.5M revenue, €1.39M EBITDA (40% margin)
Recurring route — ~2–3× ARR~€3.5–5M recurring revenuePlan Year 3: Shield €4.3M + Continuum €1.5M recurring

Translated into things we can actually count (plan-stage figures from the Revenue tab):

  • ~17 Shield retainers (~€90k each) plus ~85 audits a year at ~€20k Year-2 pricing — in one winning market, fed by this funnel.
  • ~16 delivery experts offshore, hired against signed contracts — delivery stays variable-cost, margin holds.
  • The funnel running on SEO, not just paid ads — DM's machine making lead flow compounding instead of bought.
  • Audit→Shield conversion holding ~45% — the single number the valuation stands on; the MVP's job is to prove it.
VALUATION LEVER 1

Recurring share

One-off audit revenue is valued like an agency; Shield ARR is valued 2–4×. Every point of audit→retainer conversion moves the multiple, not just the revenue.

VALUATION LEVER 2

Software share

Continuum re-rates the company from "services" toward "software" multiples — the same revenue is worth more. That's why it's built from Year 1 as internal tooling.

VALUATION LEVER 3

Founder-independence

A buyer pays for a machine, not for three founders. Playbooks, the delivery bench and the automated funnel are what make the multiple stick.

VALUATION LEVER 4

Growth rate

3.5× year-on-year growth (plan Y1→Y2) is what pushes a services multiple to the top of its range — and what no mature agency book can show.

Realistically

€10M is the plan's Year-2/Year-3 shape: ~€1.4M EBITDA or ~€4M recurring. Gate #1 is the 90-day MVP — if the funnel numbers hold, the rest is execution, not invention.
