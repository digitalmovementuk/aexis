Why now

Three forces. One segment. Same 18 months.

TECH SHOCK

Offensive AI

Agentic AI runs the whole attack chain — recon to exfiltration — with no human in the loop.

REGULATORY CLOCK

AI made attackers stronger. Regulation made defence mandatory. The talent gap made building in-house impossible.
