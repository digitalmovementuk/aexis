## 1. Executive summary

In April 2026, Anthropic's "Mythos" red-team preview showed an AI system autonomously discovering **thousands of critical software vulnerabilities** and **chaining them into working exploits in hours that would have taken expert humans weeks**. Six months earlier, the first confirmed **agentic-AI espionage operation** ran the entire attack lifecycle — reconnaissance to exfiltration — against major companies and governments. The offensive capability of AI has crossed a threshold. The defensive maturity of most organisations has not.

That gap is the business. **AEXIS is an AI-threat-native cyber-assurance company** that helps regulated mid-market organisations — banks, insurers, healthcare providers and critical-infrastructure operators with roughly 250–5,000 employees — answer one question their board and their regulator are now legally compelled to ask: *"Are we resilient against an AI-powered attacker, and can we prove it?"*

We answer it in three moves:
1. **AEXIS Signal** — a fixed-fee **AI-Threat Readiness Audit** that measures exactly what an AI-equipped attacker would find. The wedge.
2. **AEXIS Shield** — an ongoing **managed safeguarding retainer** that closes the gaps, defends continuously, and produces the compliance evidence regulators demand. The recurring core.
3. **AEXIS Continuum** — a **software platform** that productises the repeatable parts of Signal + Shield into continuous AI-threat exposure management with automated compliance evidence. The scalable future and the valuation engine.

**Why now is unusually sharp:** the technology shock (offensive AI) and the regulatory clock (NIS2 in force Dec 2025, DORA enforceable since Jan 2025, Singapore's Cybersecurity Amendment Act live Oct 2025, the UK Cyber Security & Resilience Bill in Parliament) have fired *simultaneously*, for *exactly our segment*, and made cyber resilience a **personally-liable, deadline-bound board obligation**. Meanwhile a record **4.8M-person global skills gap** means these organisations structurally **cannot hire their way out** — they must buy outcomes.

**Why us:** a commercial founder-CEO who builds and converts pipeline (the scarce skill in a category full of engineers who can't sell), paired with a senior technical co-founder/CISO who owns delivery credibility. A productised, AI-native delivery model that is faster and cheaper than the Big-4 and broader than any single-country specialist — and a **single cross-border offer (DE+UK+SG)** that no incumbent provides.

**The plan:** reach **€1.0M revenue in Year 1, €3.5M in Year 2, and €9.0M in Year 3**, exiting Year 3 with **~€8.2M of annual recurring revenue** and **EBITDA-positive**. We are raising **€2.5M** to fund ~30 months to that point.

The combined cyber market across the three countries is **~€30bn**; our serviceable segment is **~€4.5bn**; a €9M Year-3 outcome is **~0.2% of it**. This is a sales-execution story inside a structurally exploding market — not a bet on the market existing.

## 2. The problem — and why the timing is exceptional

### 2.1 Offensive AI has changed the threat model
- AI now finds and weaponises vulnerabilities at machine speed. The Mythos preview converted **N-day exploitation from weeks to hours**; the criminal ecosystem is industrialising the same capability (Flashpoint logged a **1,500% jump in AI-related illicit chatter** in a single month and **3.3 billion compromised credentials** in 2025).
- Agentic AI automates the *whole* attack chain — reconnaissance, phishing, credential-stuffing, lateral movement, exfiltration — **without a human in the loop**, collapsing the cost of a sophisticated attack and multiplying the number of capable adversaries.

### 2.2 Defenders are exposed and they know it
- **IBM (2025):** average breach **USD 4.44M**; **97%** of organisations hit by an AI-related incident **lacked AI access controls**, **63%** had **no AI governance policy**.
- **UK (2025/26):** **43%** of businesses breached last year; only **24%** of AI-adopters manage the associated risk; only **25%** hold an incident-response plan.
- **Germany (Bitkom 2025):** cyber damage of **€202.4bn/year**; **34%** hit by ransomware; **59%** say attacks threaten their existence.

### 2.3 The regulatory clock has started — for our exact segment
Within the last 18 months, in all three target markets, cyber resilience became a **legal, board-level, personally-liable obligation**:
- **Germany/EU — NIS2** (in force 6 Dec 2025, no transition): regulated entities jump from **~4,500 to ~30,000**; threshold **>€10M turnover OR ≥50 staff**; **fines up to €10M / 2% turnover**; **personal management liability**; mandatory board training.
- **EU finance — DORA** (enforceable 17 Jan 2025): ICT risk management, resilience testing, hours-fast incident reporting; **fines up to 10% turnover**; senior managers liable to **€1M**.
- **Singapore — Cybersecurity (Amendment) Act 2024** (live 31 Oct 2025): expanded CII duties, **2-hour incident reporting**, new regulated categories; **MAS TRM/Cyber-Hygiene Notices** binding on financial institutions.
- **UK — Cyber Security & Resilience Bill** (in committee, Feb 2026): brings **managed service providers and data centres into scope** for the first time.

### 2.4 They cannot staff their way out
The **ISC2 2025 study** records a **record 4.8M unfilled cyber roles (+19% YoY)**; **88%** of organisations report skills gaps and **88%** suffered a real security consequence because of them. For the first time, **budget overtook talent scarcity** as the top staffing constraint — i.e. buyers increasingly **cannot afford in-house teams and will purchase outcome-based services**.

> **The problem in one line:** AI made attackers stronger and faster; regulation made defence mandatory and personal; the talent gap made building in-house impossible. The regulated mid-market is caught in the middle — under-served by the Big-4 (too expensive) and by point-product vendors (sell tools, not assured outcomes). **That is the AEXIS customer.**

## 3. The solution — AEXIS

A single, outcome-based promise: **"Find what an AI attacker would find. Close it. Keep it closed. Prove it."** Delivered through a three-layer offer that is deliberately structured as a **land-and-expand engine**.

### 3.1 AEXIS Signal — the AI-Threat Readiness Audit *(the wedge)*
A fixed-fee, fixed-timeline (4–6 week) diagnostic that uses AI-native offensive tooling — the same class of capability the attackers now have — to measure an organisation's real exposure across its estate, identity, supply chain and (increasingly) its own AI/LLM deployments. Deliverable: a board-ready **AEXIS Exposure Score (0–100)**, a prioritised remediation plan, and a **regulatory-mapping pack** (NIS2 / DORA / SG-CSA / MAS as applicable).
- *Why it lands:* it answers the question the regulator and board are already asking, at a price a mid-market firm can sign without a procurement marathon. It creates urgency with **evidence, not fear**.
- *Average price (working assumption):* **€35,000.**

### 3.2 AEXIS Shield — managed safeguarding *(the recurring core)*
The ongoing service that turns a one-off score into continuous assurance: managed detection & response tuned for AI-speed attacks, continuous/periodic AI-driven exposure testing, remediation oversight, and **always-current compliance evidence** so the customer is audit-ready every day, not once a year.
- *Why it sticks:* compliance never expires, threats never stop, and switching means re-papering your regulator. High retention, high switching cost.
- *Average contract value (working assumption):* **€90,000/year**, with **~45%** of Signal clients converting.

### 3.3 AEXIS Continuum — the platform *(the scalable future)*
The software layer that productises the repeatable 70% of Signal + Shield: continuous exposure scoring, AI-threat simulation, and **automated, regulator-mapped evidence generation** in one dashboard. It launches as internal delivery tooling (Year 1), becomes a customer-facing portal bundled with Shield (Year 2), and is sold as a standalone subscription (Year 3+).
- *Why it matters to value:* it converts a services business (revenue-linear with headcount) into a software business (revenue-decoupled from headcount), lifting gross margin from ~55% toward ~80% and unlocking SaaS valuation multiples. It is also the data moat — every engagement trains better detection.
- *Average subscription (working assumption):* **~€30,000/year** add-on.

### 3.4 Why the hybrid sequencing is the right call
Services generate **cash, trust and proprietary data from Day 1** — funding the product build with revenue instead of pure dilution, and ensuring the product is shaped by real customer deployments rather than guesswork. This is the classic, investor-recognised "**services-led wedge → product-led scale**" path (the route taken by Palantir, and by many security firms now being acquired). It is also the only path realistically executable by a commercial-led founding team raising a seed, rather than a deep-tech team raising a large Series A to build product on spec.

The deliberately tiny penetration is the point: **AEXIS does not need the market to grow or to win share from giants — it needs to win a few hundred mid-market accounts** in a segment that is being legally compelled to buy.

### 4.2 Why these three markets
- **Germany:** largest EU economy, the deepest *Mittelstand* (high-value IP, now swept into NIS2), the highest absolute cyber damage in Europe (€202.4bn), and a cultural preference for "*Made in Germany*", evidence-based diligence — a fit for our assurance posture.
- **UK:** the largest, most mature cyber-services market in Europe (£14.7bn, 2,603 firms), English-language, fast commercial sales cycles, and a brand-new Bill pulling more buyers into scope.
- **Singapore:** the fastest-growing of the three (**16% CAGR**), the regulatory and financial gateway to APAC, English-language, with an active enforcer (MAS) and a government actively funding the sector. It de-risks geographic concentration and opens a second continent without a second language.

The three share enough (English-operable, regulation-driven, mid-market-rich) to run **one playbook**, and differ enough to **diversify regulatory and macro risk**. Many mid-market financial and infrastructure groups operate across two or three of these hubs and currently must stitch together separate local vendors — **AEXIS's single cross-border offer is a wedge no national incumbent matches.**

## 6. Business model & pricing

**Revenue streams**
1. **Signal audits** — fixed fee, **~€35k** average. Wedge; near-zero CAC for the retainer that follows.
2. **Shield retainers** — recurring, **~€90k/year** ACV; **~45%** audit-to-retainer conversion; the profit core.
3. **Continuum subscriptions** — **~€30k/year** add-on (Year 2+); the margin and multiple expander.

**Unit economics (targets by Year 3)**
- Blended gross margin **~58%** and rising as Continuum mixes in (services ~55%, platform ~80%).
- **Audit-funded CAC:** because Signal is profitable on its own, customer acquisition for the high-value retainer is effectively pre-paid by the customer — **CAC payback < 12 months**.
- **Net revenue retention** target **≥115%** (retainer uplift + Continuum cross-sell).
- **LTV/CAC ≥ 4×** by Year 3.
- **Rule of 40:** Year 3 growth (~157%) + EBITDA margin (~15%) ≫ 40.

**Why margins are defensible:** the recurring base is compliance-anchored (sticky), the platform decouples revenue from headcount, and AI-native delivery means each consultant covers more accounts than a classical-MDR competitor — a structural cost advantage over the national pure-plays.

## 7. Go-to-market

**Motion:** founder-led, sales-led, outbound + referral — the CEO's core competency. We sell to the **CISO, CFO/COO and Audit/Risk committee** with a single message: *measurable resilience + regulatory evidence, fast.*

**Sequenced GTM**
- **Phase 1 (Mo 0–9) — land the wedge, DE + UK.** Founder-led outbound into regulated mid-market; "AI-Threat Readiness Audit" as the door-opener riding the NIS2/DORA deadline anxiety. Target **18 audits**, first retainers, 3–4 lighthouse logos.
- **Phase 2 (Mo 9–24) — convert and expand; open Singapore.** Build the Shield recurring base, launch Continuum to existing customers, stand up the Singapore entity and first APAC hires. Channel partnerships with auditors, insurers and compliance consultancies who have the relationships but not the capability.
- **Phase 3 (Mo 24–36) — scale the engine.** Repeatable sales team, Continuum sold standalone, cross-border group accounts, category brand-building (thought leadership on AI-threat assurance).

**Demand levers that lower our cost of sales**
- **Regulatory deadlines** create inbound urgency we don't have to manufacture.
- **The audit is the marketing** — a paid, high-trust first engagement, not a free pilot.
- **Cyber insurers and auditors** become referral channels: they need their clients de-risked and have no in-house AI-threat capability.
- **Founder brand + content:** the CEO's existing operator network and a deliberate thought-leadership programme ("what an AI attacker actually does to a mid-market bank") establish category authority cheaply.

### 7.1 Growth engine — SEO & demand generation (Digital Movement UK)
A structural advantage few security start-ups have: **Digital Movement UK, the founder's own marketing agency, builds AEXIS a steady, low-cost stream of inbound leads from Google and AI search — in-house, from Day 1, at no agency fee.** Most early-stage security firms burn cash on paid ads and cold outreach; AEXIS lowers its cost-per-customer instead.

- **The demand is already searching.** Because of the new laws, buyers now search for exactly what we sell — *"NIS2 audit", "DORA testing provider", "AI threat assessment", "ISO 27001 for mid-sized firms"* — in German and English. We build the page that answers each search, per topic, per country (German for DE; English for UK + SG). A searcher with a compliance deadline is a warm, ready-to-buy lead.
- **Built for AI search (GEO), not just Google.** Buyers increasingly ask AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) instead of searching the old way. Digital Movement has optimised for AI-search citation since 2025; AEXIS content is structured to be *quoted* by these assistants — on-brand for an "AI-era" security firm that is itself found through AI.
- **It compounds, ads don't.** Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying; rankings and content are an asset that keeps delivering and grows cheaper per lead over time. Year 1 we plant the content; by Year 3 we target a large share of new audits sourced from inbound — the cheapest channel there is. This underpins the sub-12-month CAC payback and the sales efficiency in the financial model.
- **Credibility behind it:** Digital Movement's ~7-year track record (300+ clients, near-perfect retention) is the capability that makes this a real lever, not a hope. *(Stated qualitatively; no specific traffic/lead figures are claimed pre-launch.)*

The simple programme: authority content (clear guides like *"What an AI attacker does to a mid-market bank — and how to stop it"*) → topic-and-country search pages mapped to each law/service → the paid AI-Threat Readiness Audit as the single conversion offer on every page → measured monthly (rankings, traffic, leads) and doubled-down where it works.

### 11.1 Minimum capital — the floor vs the plan
We stress-tested the plan to find the smallest amount that still reaches profitability:

| Scenario | Amount | What it delivers |
|---|---|---|
| **Hard floor** | **€1.5M** | Do **not** start below this. Under €1.5M we cannot both hire the technical co-founder *and* survive the ~2-year investment dip before profit — the fastest way to lose a good business. |
| **Minimum viable** | **€1.7M** | The lean path: security co-founder + a small delivery team, **Germany + UK only** at first, Singapore later and slower, tighter hiring, leaner Continuum build. Still reaches break-even — but with little margin for error. |
| **Recommended (the raise)** | **€2.5M** | The minimum **plus** a real safety buffer past break-even, a faster Continuum build, **Singapore sooner**, accreditations, and a working-capital cushion for services receivables. |

**The €0.8M difference (€2.5M − €1.7M) is buffer and acceleration, not survival money.** Raising to €2.5M is a deliberate risk-reduction choice: it removes single-point timing risk and pulls the high-margin software revenue (and the second market) forward.

## 12. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **"AI defence eventually favours defenders, urgency fades."** | Anthropic itself calls the transition *tumultuous* and multi-year. AEXIS sells both the transition **and** the steady state — compliance and continuous assurance never expire. |
| **Big-4 / national pure-plays move on the mid-market.** | Our cost structure (AI-native delivery), speed, productisation and the **cross-border single-vendor** offer are hard for an enterprise-priced or single-country incumbent to copy quickly. |
| **Platform giants (CrowdStrike/Palo Alto) bundle "AI security."** | They sell product to secure the AI stack; we sell assured outcomes + compliance for the whole estate. We **partner with / resell** their telemetry and own the layer they won't staff. |
| **Audits commoditise.** | The audit is the wedge, not the business; value compounds in the recurring Shield base and the Continuum data moat. |
| **Founder-led sales doesn't scale past the CEO.** | The audit-as-wedge motion is documentable and repeatable; Phase-3 funds a trained sales team; productisation reduces the selling complexity. |
| **Key-person / technical-cofounder dependency.** | First and largest call on the raise; de-risked by advisory bench and accreditation processes that institutionalise the methodology. |
| **Three-market operational complexity.** | All three are English-operable and regulation-aligned; one playbook, staged rollout (DE+UK first, SG in Phase 2), local entity + country lead before scaling each. |
| **Regulation softens.** | The drivers are **enacted law today** (NIS2, DORA, SG-CSA live; UK Bill advanced), not anticipated — demand is anchored in current obligations. |

As AI makes attacks cheaper, faster and more numerous, *assurance* becomes the scarce, recurring, compliance-mandated product every regulated organisation must buy, forever.

AEXIS intends to be the **default name for AI-era cyber assurance in the regulated mid-market** — starting in three of the world's most demanding regulatory markets, built on a services wedge, scaled on a software platform, and trusted because everything it claims, it can prove.
