# AEXIS — Brand & Design System

**Working name:** AEXIS *(pending trademark + domain clearance — see note below)*
**Tagline:** *Security assurance for the AI era.*
**Category line:** AI-threat-native cyber assurance for the regulated mid-market.

---

## 1. Brand idea — "Calm authority in a market built on fear"
Most cyber brands sell with red alerts and panic. AEXIS does the opposite. **Threat is the context; assurance is the product.** The brand projects the composure of a firm that has already measured the risk, fixed it, and can prove it — the posture of Accenture/McKinsey applied to the AI-era security problem.

- **Positioning:** Assurance, not alarm.
- **Personality:** Precise · human · forward-looking.
- **Promise:** Everything we claim, we can show — on a dashboard and in a compliance file.

## 2. Name rationale
**AEXIS** = *Aegis* (the shield of protection) + *Axis* (the strategic pivot point). It is short, abstract, ownable, pronounceable across EN/DE/SG-English, and free of the "skull-and-lock" cliché. Like *Accenture* ("accent on the future") it is a coined, optimistic, forward word rather than a literal description.

> **Honest note for the investor pack:** AEXIS is a strong working name but **must be cleared for trademark and `.com` availability before launch**. Two pre-vetted fallbacks carried in reserve: **VERIS** (Latin *veritas* — proof/trust) and **SENTIRA** (sentinel/sense). Naming is a Day-1 legal task, not a creative blocker.

## 3. Logo
A geometric glyph reading three ways: an **A** (AEXIS), a **shield** (aegis/protection), an upward **chevron** (forward momentum). A teal crossbar = the "measured line we hold."
- **Construction:** rounded-square ink tile (#0B0F17), violet→indigo gradient glyph, single teal crossbar.
- **Clear space:** = tile height on all sides. **Min size:** mark 24px, lockup 96px wide.
- **Don'ts:** no serif substitute, no recolouring outside violet→indigo, no shadows/skew, no skull/lock imagery.

## 4. Colour — light by default, dark for emphasis
The system is a **light theme** (white + cool grey = 90% of surfaces, signalling clarity/trust). **Ink-dark panels** are deployed deliberately to make one point land — a headline stat, a key claim. Violet = brand signal; teal = "measured/good"; alarm colours sparingly, never decorative.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Authority Ink | `#0B0F17` | Primary text · emphasis panels |
| Signal Violet | `#6B2BF6` | Primary accent · CTAs · links |
| Deep Indigo | `#3D1FD6` | Gradient end · hover |
| Measured Teal | `#00C2A8` | Positive / "resilient" signal |
| Cool Surface | `#F4F6FA` | Section backgrounds |
| Muted Slate | `#5A6473` | Secondary text |
| Caution Amber | `#FFB020` | Warnings (sparingly) |
| Critical Rose | `#FF4D6D` | Critical only (never decorative) |

## 5. Typography — two sans + one mono, no serif ever
- **Display / headlines:** Space Grotesk (600/700), tight tracking (−0.02 to −0.03em).
- **Body / UI:** Inter (400/500/600).
- **Instrument voice (eyebrows, data labels, metrics):** Space Mono (700), letter-spaced uppercase.
- **Hard rule:** never a serif (consistent with the house web-design rule).

## 6. Voice & tone — "spoken like Accenture, proven like an engineer"
Confident, optimistic, plain. Treat the AI-threat shift as a change to **get ahead of**, not a catastrophe to fear. Short imperative headlines. Outcomes before features. Never fear-sell, never bury the reader in acronyms, never claim what we can't show.

**Tone rule:** *Threat is the weather; assurance is what we sell.* Acknowledge the storm in one line, then spend the rest on the umbrella, the forecast, and the proof you're dry. Every artefact should leave the reader feeling **more in control, not less.**

## 7. UI kit
Pill badges for status; soft-shadowed cards on light surfaces; ink panels for emphasis; rounded 100px buttons; generous whitespace. Stat cards (big Space-Grotesk number + Space-Mono label) are the workhorse for dashboards and decks. Mobile: centre-aligned.

## 8. Application
The design system runs through **every** AEXIS artefact — business plan, investor deck, financial model and product UI — so the company looks like one confident, coherent operator from the first touch.
