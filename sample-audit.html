The attack story

How these add up to a breach — step by step.

On their own, the findings look manageable. Chained together by an AI attacker, they become one fast, quiet break-in.

MINUTES

Find the open door

An AI tool scans Nordlicht's internet edge and spots the unpatched remote-access gateway among thousands of systems.

HOURS

Build the break-in

The AI writes a working exploit on its own — the exact capability Anthropic's "Mythos" showed in April 2026 — and gets inside.

SAME DAY

Sign in as an admin

Using passwords from public leak dumps and weak two-factor, it reaches an admin account and gains real control.

SAME DAY

Reach the money systems

Over-powered service accounts let it move sideways into the payments platform and the client database.

ONGOING

Steal data — twice over

It quietly copies client data out. Meanwhile, staff pasting data into public AI tools is leaking more, every day.

THE COST

A reportable incident

A DORA major incident: a 2-hour reporting clock, possible fines up to 10% of turnover, and damaged client trust.

The fix-it plan

90 days to a much safer position.

Prioritised by impact. The first 30 days remove the critical risks; the rest builds lasting resilience.

DAYS 0–30 · STOP THE BLEEDING

Close the critical gaps

  • Patch the remote-access gateway now
  • Set clear rules + safe tooling for staff AI use
  • Turn on phishing-resistant sign-in for all admins
DAYS 31–60 · TIGHTEN

Cut the attacker's options

  • Lock down over-powered service accounts
  • Review the payments supplier's security
  • Add AI-speed attack detection
DAYS 61–90 · PROVE IT

Resilient and audit-ready

  • Test the incident-response plan against an AI attack
  • Close the DORA & NIS2 evidence gaps
  • Switch on continuous monitoring (AEXIS Shield)
