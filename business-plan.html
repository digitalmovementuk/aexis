Change 1 · Technology

AI can attack on its own

New AI tools run a whole attack by themselves — finding targets, breaking in, and stealing data — with no human needed. The first real case hit big firms and governments in late 2025.

Change 2 · Law

Defence is now the law

Across all three of our countries, new rules now force companies to protect themselves. Fines reach 2–10% of yearly turnover, and bosses can be held personally responsible.

Change 3 · People

They can't hire enough

There are 4.8 million empty cyber jobs. Most companies also can't afford big in-house teams. The only realistic option left is to buy the result as a service.

Ads stop the day you stop paying.

Search rankings and content are an asset that keeps working. The more we publish, the more leads arrive — at no extra cost. Our cost to win a customer goes down over time, while rivals' ad costs go up.

The compounding effect.

Year 1 we plant the content. By Year 3 we aim for a large share of new check-ups to come from search — the cheapest channel there is. This is built into the strong sales-efficiency numbers in our plan.

The simple plan

  • Authority content: clear guides like "What an AI attacker does to a mid-sized bank — and how to stop it." This builds trust and rankings.
  • Search pages per topic and country: one strong page for each law and service, in the right language for each market.
  • The check-up as the offer: every page leads the reader to book a paid AI-Threat Readiness Audit — our way in.
  • Measured every month: we track rankings, visits and leads, and double down on what works.
