AEXIS makes cyber resilience visible, measured and trusted in the AI era. The brand is built to feel like the calm authority in a loud, fearful market — clean, technical, human. Light by default, decisive in the dark.

01 — Brand idea

Calm authority in a market built on fear.

Most cyber brands sell with red alerts, skulls and panic. AEXIS does the opposite. Threat is the context; assurance is the product. The brand projects the composure of a firm that has already measured the risk, fixed it, and can prove it — the posture of Accenture or McKinsey, applied to the AI-era security problem.

Positioning

Assurance, not alarm

We lead with the outcome — "you are provably resilient" — never with the scare. Confidence is the differentiator in a noisy category.

Personality

Precise · human · forward

Engineer-grade precision, spoken in plain language. Optimistic about the future the way Accenture is: change is an opportunity, and we help you meet it.

Promise

Make it measurable

Everything we claim, we can show on a dashboard and in a compliance file. The brand never asserts what it can't evidence.

02 — Logo

The mark: a shield, an "A", a forward step.

The glyph reads three ways at once — an A for AEXIS, a shield (aegis) for protection, and an upward chevron for forward momentum. The teal crossbar is the "measured" signal: the line we hold. Always paired with the wordmark in tight, confident tracking.

Clear space

Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark's rounded tile on all sides. Never crowd the lockup.

Minimum size

Mark min 24px; full lockup min 96px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

Don'ts

No serif substitute, no recolouring the glyph outside violet→indigo, no drop shadows, no stretching, no skull/lock clichés.

03 — Colour

Light by default. Dark for emphasis.

The system is a light theme — white and cool-grey surfaces carry 90% of the experience, signalling clarity and trust. Ink-dark panels are used deliberately, to make a single point land: a headline stat, a key claim, a moment of authority. Violet is the brand signal; teal is the "measured / good" accent; alarm colours are used sparingly and never decoratively.

04 — Typography

Two sans, one mono. No serif, ever.

Space Grotesk carries every headline — a technical grotesque with just enough character to feel modern, not corporate-default. Inter handles all body and UI for maximum legibility. Space Mono is the "instrument" voice — eyebrows, data labels, metrics — signalling precision and measurement.

05 — Voice & tone

Spoken like Accenture. Proven like an engineer.

Confident, optimistic, plain. We treat the AI-threat shift as a change to get ahead of, not a catastrophe to fear. Short imperative headlines. Outcomes before features. Never fear-sell, never bury the reader in acronyms, never claim what we can't show.

✕ Not this

"Catastrophic AI-powered APT threats could devastate your enterprise. Act now before it's too late!"

✓ This

"AI changed who can attack you, and how fast. We measure your exposure, close it, and keep it closed — and we can prove every step."

✕ Not this

"Leverage our synergistic next-gen cyber-resilience paradigm and holistic threat-intel fabric."

✓ This

"One team. Three things: find what an AI attacker would find, fix it, and give you the evidence your regulator and board need."

✕ Not this

"You are probably already breached and don't know it. Fear is the only rational response."

✓ This

"Most mid-market firms can't yet see their AI-era exposure. That's a gap, not a verdict — and it's fixable. Here's the number, and here's the plan."

Tone rule

Threat is the weather. Assurance is what we sell.

We acknowledge the storm in one line, then spend the rest of the conversation on the umbrella, the forecast and the proof that you're dry. Every AEXIS artefact should leave the reader feeling more in control, not less.
