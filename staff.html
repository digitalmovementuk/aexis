Who delivers each product.

The people and roles behind Signal, Shield and Continuum — what the three founders cover in-house, what we automate, and the one role we hire.

01 — Our staffing model

Lean by design: founders in-house, hire only against signed work.

The three founders cover strategy, technical delivery and the digital side themselves — so there's no fixed payroll. The only delivery hire is a senior cybersecurity specialist, brought on against signed contracts, so it stays a variable cost that revenue already covers.
