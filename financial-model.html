Why €2.5M, not less

Minimum viable need is ~€1.7M (see the dedicated section in the business plan). We raise €2.5M to (1) hold a full safety margin past breakeven, (2) absorb services receivables timing, (3) fund three-country entities and accreditations (ISO 27001, CREST/CHECK-equivalent — themselves a competitive moat), and (4) accelerate the Continuum build so the software margin arrives sooner. Buffer is de-risking, not padding.
