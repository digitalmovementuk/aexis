01 — The three revenue streams

Land with a project. Grow into a relationship. Scale with software.

Each stream does a different job. Together they turn a single audit into a long, expanding customer relationship.

Stream 1 · Land

Signal — the audit

One-off project revenue

The paid AI-threat vulnerability audit. Opens the door, builds trust, and produces the data behind everything after it.

From €7,500 (Yr 1 entry) → €35k at market rate
Stream 2 · Expand

Shield — managed safeguarding

Recurring revenue

The audit becomes an ongoing relationship — continuous protection and always-current compliance evidence. The sticky core.

~€90k / year · ~45% convert
Stream 3 · Scale

Continuum — the platform

Software subscription

The repeatable parts of delivery become software, so revenue stops being tied to people's time.

~€30k / year

05 — Pricing strategy

Price low to get in. Raise as we prove value.

Year 1 is about winning logos and data, not margin. We enter below market — under the level a CISO can approve without procurement — then ramp to the market rate the research supports.

YEAR 1 · ENTRY

€7,500–9,500

Founding-client rate. Sub-€10k means a fast yes, no procurement. Wins the first cohort and Shield conversions — still profitable on offshore delivery.

YEAR 2 · PROVEN

~€20,000

Backed by case studies and outcomes — the middle of the market range for a comprehensive audit.

YEAR 3 · MARKET

€30,000–45,000

Full market rate for regulated mid-market audits.

Grounded in 2025–26 pricing: mid-market penetration tests / security audits run ~$10k–$50k, with 150–500-staff firms budgeting $20k–$50k. Year-1 entry pricing trades margin for speed of acquisition.

06 — Capital

Capital-light by design. A raise is optional, not survival.

Key point

Because we're profitable from the first contract, we don't need outside money to survive. Any capital simply pours fuel on the market that's already proven to convert.

BOOTSTRAP PATH

€0 raised — self-funding

The Meta MVP plus offshore delivery can fund its own growth. Slower, but fully founder-owned and de-risked.

OPTIONAL ACCELERATOR

A small raise to go faster

Once the MVP names the winning market, an optional raise scales Meta spend, the offshore delivery bench, and the Continuum platform — to capture the market before anyone else.
